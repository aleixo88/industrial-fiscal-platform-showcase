# Controlled Evaluation Process

The proprietary product is evaluated through progressive disclosure. Public
documentation provides context; confidential evidence is shared only after the
interested party and evaluation scope are qualified.

## Suggested process

1. Public review of this showcase.
2. Introductory conversation about strategic fit and intended transaction.
3. Confidentiality agreement when appropriate.
4. Controlled product demonstration using sanitized or synthetic data.
5. Technical due diligence with supervised evidence access.
6. Commercial proposal covering asset scope, transfer and transition support.
7. Definitive agreements and controlled handover.

## Evidence available in a private process

- source-tree and architecture walkthrough;
- database schema and migration history;
- automated test and gate reports;
- controlled release-candidate inspection;
- installation, backup and recovery evidence;
- known limitations and external dependencies;
- product roadmap and transfer plan;
- supervised access to selected proprietary code.

## Not provided publicly

- unrestricted source-code access;
- production databases, backups or customer documents;
- credentials, certificates or external-provider secrets;
- company-specific fiscal parameters;
- identifiable screenshots or operational exports.

## Contact boundary

Initial contact should be made through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Public
issues are not an appropriate channel for confidential information. A serious
evaluation should identify the interested organization, its intended use and
the people responsible for technical and commercial review.
