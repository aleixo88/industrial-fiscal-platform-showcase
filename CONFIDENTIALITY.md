# Public Disclosure and Confidentiality Boundary

This repository is a sanitized technical showcase.

## Publicly disclosed

- problem domain and product thesis;
- high-level architecture;
- generalized capability descriptions;
- internal engineering evidence and its limitations;
- current maturity and remaining external validation frontier.

## Not publicly disclosed

- source code or complete internal documentation;
- internal product or company names;
- client, supplier, employee or partner identities;
- tax identifiers, document keys or transaction-level records;
- production screenshots containing identifiable information;
- database files, backups or operational exports;
- credentials, tokens, passwords, certificates or secret references;
- company-specific fiscal rules and governed tax parameters;
- private repository paths, workstation paths or environment details.

## Due diligence

Additional evidence may be shared only through a controlled private process,
with scope appropriate to the evaluator and confidentiality protections when
required.

Public issues must not be used to send confidential, operational or security
information.
